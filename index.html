Studio Guidelines

Policies & Booking Terms

Clear agreements keep expectations mutual and transparent. All confirmed bookings adhere to these standards.

01

Retainer & Date Reservation

A non-refundable 50% retainer deposit is required at the time of booking to officially secure your date and time on the calendar.

No shoot dates are reserved without receipt of this deposit and confirmation agreement.

02

Cancellation & Refund Terms

If a client cancels a session with at least 48 hours prior written notice, any amount paid beyond the initial retainer will be refunded, up to a maximum refund of 75% of the total session fee.

The remaining 25% represents the non-refundable reservation deposit covering scheduled calendar exclusivity and preparation. Cancellations made with less than 48 hours notice forfeit all fees paid.

03

Houston Weather & Rescheduling

Because sessions are held outdoors on location, severe weather (thunderstorms, extreme flash floods, or high winds) can affect shoots.

If conditions are unsafe or unworkable, your 50% retainer transfers 100% to a rescheduled date within 60 days at no added charge.

04

Image Rights & Delivery

Clients receive high-resolution, professionally color-graded JPEG files via private online download link within 7 business days.

Every session includes non-commercial personal print and social media sharing rights. Unedited RAW files are not released under any circumstance.

Frequently Asked Questions

We shoot across public parks, cultural districts, and open-access scenic spots around Houston (e.g., Buffalo Bayou, Hermann Park, The Heights, Downtown architectural streets). Texas weather is unpredictable. We evaluate the forecast 24 hours prior. If rain or heavy weather threatens the shoot, we will coordinate a mutual reschedule date at zero penalty.

For shoots with 2 or 3 looks, we recommend quick-layer garments, or choosing locations with public restrooms or nearby parked vehicles for convenient changes.
